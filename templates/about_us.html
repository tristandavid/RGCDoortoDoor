The story behind RGC Door-to-Door Box Express Services

Our Story

RGC Door-to-Door Box Express Services to the Philippines officially started in January 2024, but the journey behind it goes back much further.

Our founder began as a part-time box picker in the summer of 2019, working independently for Golden Island Freight and using his own Tundra pickup truck to collect boxes from families across the Greater Toronto Area. From there, he went on to work with LuzViMin Door to Door Box and RP XPRESS, learning the ins and outs of the balikbayan box business from the ground up.

In late 2023, when LuzViMin and RP XPRESS parted ways over rising drop-off fees, our founder saw an opportunity to do things his own way — with fair pricing, honest service, and a genuine care for every box and every family behind it. That opportunity became RGC.
